Become a Seaworthy Contributor!

You've gained access to this amazing archive thanks to the efforts of many who came before you, contributing to its completion. To keep this project alive and growing for everyone, we encourage you to be a responsible member of our community!

Here's how you can help complete the archive for future generations:

  • Share Missing Assets: The archive uses a smart proxy technology. When you play a game and it tries to load a file that's not yet in your local archive, our system intelligently downloads it from the original website for you. Crucially, it also reports the URL of that missing file back to us (without any personal data!). This helps us identify and add these assets to the main archive, making it more complete and eventually fully offline for everyone. By simply playing, you contribute!
  • Pass It On: Just as others helped you get this archive, you can help others discover and access it. By keeping the launcher running, you make the archive available for others to download directly from you. It's a way of paying it forward and ensuring this collection continues to thrive in the community.

Your participation helps ensure the long-term preservation and accessibility of these beloved games. Thank you for being a part of it!
